Evidence Check

Use the original download. Maximum size 64 MiB. Copy the 64-character fingerprint from the trusted case record or source manifest.

After the first check, change one character in a copy of the file and compare again.

What does a match tell me?

The fingerprint identifies the file’s bytes. A match lets you record that your copy agrees with the trusted reference. Check the source, date, coverage and interpretation separately.
