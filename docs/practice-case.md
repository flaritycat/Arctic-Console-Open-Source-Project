# P04: Northern Bay practice assignment

Edition 1, 1 November 2026. Newly authored fictional material for this public project. No private application code, diagrams or scientific observations are used.

## Assignment shown to the analyst

A report says the average ice concentration at surveyed points rose from 40% to 60%. Decide what that supports about Northern Bay, explain a limitation and give the next analyst a useful next check.

Northern Bay, its observations and dates are invented for training. The exercise makes no claim about a real Arctic location. It teaches the difference between a comparison, its geographic scope and a defensible assessment.

## Evidence

**E1 — fictional observation table:** on 30 October 2026, points A, B and C report 20%, 40% and 60%. On 31 October, different points D and E report 50% and 70%. The arithmetic means are 40% and 60%.

**E2 — sampling note:** none of the first day's locations was revisited. There are only five fictional readings, no bay-wide coverage and no uncertainty estimate. A change between these sample means does not establish a change at matched locations or across the whole bay.

Both sources are embedded in the application. Their exact text is hashed for saved-record reference checks. A file check does not validate the analyst's judgment or authenticate its author.

## Screen sequence and completion

1. **Alert:** explain the assignment and present one primary action, Open the alert.
2. **Evidence:** show two readable sources with their date, meaning and limitation. The analyst opens each and deliberately marks it reviewed. Evidence can be revisited.
3. **Assessment:** start empty. Ask for a decision, supported finding, limitation and next check. Explain missing requirements next to the action. Notes remain present when navigating or recovering from an import error.
4. **Handover:** show the exact assessment before preparing a JSON file. State clearly that preparing/downloading it does not send it or transfer responsibility.
5. **Reader:** open the handover in a fresh session. Check its structure, checksum and references against this case edition; show the author's words and both sources. Finish the reader exercise explicitly as local training, without suggesting a live acknowledgement.

A draft file may contain incomplete fields. A handover requires both sources reviewed, a decision and nonblank finding, limitation and next check. These are structural requirements, not automated scientific grading. The reader can inspect any structurally valid decision, including an incorrect one.

## Recovery, privacy and accessibility

No backend or persistent browser storage. Drafts exist in memory until explicitly downloaded; users can import their draft to resume. Replacing existing work requires confirmation. Failed, oversized, changed, unsupported or wrong-kind imports preserve the current work. Starting over requires confirmation after work has begun.

Files are capped at 64 KiB. Imported text is rendered as text, never executable HTML. Checksums are unkeyed integrity checks, not signatures. Personal names are not required. The data-handling notice covers local files, downloaded records and hosted-page requests.

Use semantic headings, visible focus, labelled fields, inline errors and focus management after navigation. Keep the sequence usable at 320 CSS px and 200% zoom. Expandable details carry optional teaching and technical content.

## Facilitator review

Ask the author what the 20 percentage-point difference describes. A whole-bay change claim or treating a fingerprint match as proof of an interpretation is a critical misunderstanding. A defensible next check would revisit comparable locations or obtain evidence with suitable coverage and uncertainty.

Observe a second reader independently. Ask whether anyone has actually received or accepted responsibility through this tool. The correct answer is that this local exercise does not perform live delivery or ownership transfer.

Automated tests establish workflow behavior only. P06's observed-participant threshold remains open; no completion rate is inferred from this specification or browser rehearsal.
