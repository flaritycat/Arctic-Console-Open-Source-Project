# Northern Bay: your shift assignment

Offline: open `practice.html` in the extracted release; for the next shift choose **Review a handover**. You only need a browser. Use fictional information. The person running the session will tell you which role to try. You can stop at any time.

**First shift:** [Open the practice](https://flaritycat.github.io/Arctic-Console-Open-Source-Project/practice.html). A colleague flagged a change in Northern Bay. Decide what the next shift can rely on and what it should do next. Leave something another analyst could pick up. Tell the observer when you think you are done.

**Next shift:** Your observer will give you a file. [Open the reader](https://flaritycat.github.io/Arctic-Console-Open-Source-Project/practice.html?reader=1). Decide what you would rely on in the file, what you would check next and where the assignment stands now. Tell the observer when you think you are done.

If something is unclear, say what you expected. There is no need to post your file or answers online.
